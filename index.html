The evolution of
renewable energy systems

For almost 150 years, solar and wind were lab curiosities, too expensive for the grid. Then solar's cost fell about 90% in the 2010s, and by 2020 the IEA called it the cheapest electricity in history. This timeline follows that arc, spaced to scale by year, so the long quiet stretches and the sudden acceleration both read at a glance. Click any milestone to see what happened under the hood.

Notes: disputed or commonly-mistaken claims

Several "facts" repeated across popular sources are wrong or contested. This timeline uses the corrected versions below.

The 1839 discoverer was Edmond Becquerel
Not his father Antoine-César, nor his son Henri, who discovered radioactivity. The three are often confused.
The "first" wind turbine is contested
James Blyth (Scotland, 1887) vs. Charles Brush (Cleveland, 1888). Brush's is best described as the first automatically operated one.
La Rance's capacity factor is 24 to 28%
Not the 40% some promotional sources claim.
The Philippines is now the 3rd-largest geothermal producer
Indonesia overtook it around 2018; it was long #2. As of 2024: US #1, Indonesia #2, Philippines #3.
